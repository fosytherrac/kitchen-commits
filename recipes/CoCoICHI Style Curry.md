# CoCoICHI Style Curry

*Intended for 3 People*

### Ingredients

Butter = +/- 45g
Ginger = 2 tiny slices of a root
Flour = 3 Table spoons
Potatoes = 3
Carrots = 2
Apple juice = 1dl
Garlic Clove = 1
Onions = 1 
Peanut- or Rapeseed-Oil (10/10 naming btw)
Soy Sauce = 5 Table spoons
Chicken Broth = 1dl
Curry (Spice)
Garam Masala Mix (Spice)

#### Note

You could use **Corn flour** instead of the Potatoes and Carrots if you want to be faster (not tested, but i am sure it will work).

If you want to use the Potatoes and Carrots, make sure to have a **Sieve**.

### Prep

Chop the Carrots and Potatoes and Garlic Clove into small pieces (very small)

### Steps

1. Heat the pan on **medium heat** with Oil in the pan.
2. Put the Onions, Garlic and Ginger into the pan.
3. Brown them a little bit for **3-5 minutes**, make sure they are just a tad brown.
4. Add Butter and the Flour and let it get brown without burning it.
5. Add Apple juice and Chicken Broth
6. Add Soy Sauce
7. Add the Spices until it tastes like it should (you have to experiment).
